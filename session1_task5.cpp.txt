Comparison: C vs OOP

Data and functions were separate in C.

In OOP, data and methods are combined inside a class, making the program easier to manage.
Managing task status was difficult in C.
In OOP, the markTaskDone() method easily changes the task status to DONE.
Managing multiple tasks required more code in C.
In OOP, methods like addTask(), markTaskDone(), and showTasks() make task management easier.
