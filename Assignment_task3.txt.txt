Algorithm : ordering food on swiggy 

1. open the swiggy application.
2. log in to your account.
3. enter or confirm your delivery location.
4. search for a restaurant or food item.
5. select the restaurant.
6. choose the food items you want to order.
7. add the selected items to the cart.
8. review your cart and click "proceed to checkout."
9. select or confirm the delivery address.
10. choose payment method.
11. complete the payment.
12. your food order is placed successfully, and you can track the delivery.
